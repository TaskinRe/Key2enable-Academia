# Marketing content and asset sources

Verified 21 November 2026.

- Network: https://www.key2enable.ae/labs lists 8 countries, 10+ UAE locations, and 1,499+ students reached. These are organisation-reported reach figures, not trial outcomes or a worldwide hub count.
- ZHO: https://www.za.gov.ae/en/Media-Centre/News/Zayed-higher-organization-launches-naqra-lab-in-abu-ahabi-and-al-ain announces two Naqra labs in Abu Dhabi and Al Ain, February 2025, in collaboration with Key2Enable. This does not establish that Key2Enable is owned by ZHO.
- Case study: https://nafath.mada.org.qa/nafath-article/mcn3005/ reports 50 learners over four months, with average attendance approximately 65% before and over 90% afterward. This is an observational case study; no causal or individual outcome guarantee is implied. Authors Sabrin Shaikh, Yusra Sayed, Ernest Sahakyan, Tatevik Muradyan are credited as research authors, not assigned staff titles.
